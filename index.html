A STUDENT PROJECT · CURRENTLY IN DEVELOPMENT

Getting a few pages printed shouldn't take half your break.

TimeToPrint is an idea for making campus printing a little less frustrating. Upload a document from your phone, choose your print settings, pay, and collect your prints when they're ready.

I'm exploring how a simple self-service printing kiosk could make the whole process easier for students.

What I'm aiming for

A few things this idea needs to get right.

01

Less time waiting

Let students start a print request from their phone instead of joining a queue first.

02

Clear pricing

Make it easy to review copies, print settings, and the expected cost before confirming.

03

Careful with files

Work out how uploaded documents should be handled and deleted safely.

These are goals for the concept, not guarantees of a live printing service.

The problem

Printing should be the easy part of an assignment.

Finding time to print an assignment, a form or a report can be frustrating, especially when a deadline is close. These are the points of friction I'm trying to understand better. How much they matter will vary from campus to campus.

01

Waiting at the wrong time

Printing can become inconvenient when several students need it around the same deadline.

02

Unclear printing steps

Students should be able to review their document and settings before confirming.

03

Questions about file handling

Students may want clarity about how documents are accessed and retained.

04

Not knowing what's happening

A clear status would make the process easier to understand.

How it works

How it could work

The idea is to make a print request possible from a phone, without making the process complicated.

  1. 01

    Scan

    Scan a QR code on the proposed kiosk to open the printing page.

  2. 02

    Upload

    Choose a document and review the file before moving ahead.

  3. 03

    Set your preferences

    Choose the number of copies, colour or black-and-white printing, and other available settings.

  4. 04

    Review and pay

    See the expected cost before confirming. UPI payment is a proposed part of the concept, not an implemented integration.

  5. 05

    Collect

    The intended experience is to let students know when their prints are ready and where to collect them.

Open questions

What's still being figured out.

Before this is anything more than an idea, these details need to be designed and tested. I'm not claiming any of them are solved yet.

Files, payments and failures

  • How long should uploaded documents be kept?
  • How would deletion be implemented and verified?
  • Who could access a file, and how is that limited?
  • What should happen when payment fails?
  • What happens if printing fails or the paper runs out?

Possible building blocks

  • A kiosk enclosure and printer
  • A QR-based browser interface
  • A possible payment integration, with UPI as the proposed option
  • A way to monitor job status

These are components to explore. They are not described here as built or verified.
